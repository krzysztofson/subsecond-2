# subsecond — LinkedIn Content System & 100-Post Calendar

Personal LinkedIn profile, first-person founder voice, English only. 3 posts/week (Mon/Wed/Fri), paired with a Canva graphic or screenshot per the visual note on each post. All facts, metrics, and quotes below trace back to the live site copy (`index.html` / `src/i18n.ts`) — case studies are anonymized (no client names exist in the source copy) but the metrics are real, verified results.

## 1. Pillars

## 2. Weekly cadence

Three posts a week, loosely themed but not rigidly locked to a pillar-per-day formula (real calendars need flexibility):
- **Monday — The Mirror:** diagnosis and case-study Leak/Insight/Result angles. Opens the week with recognition.
- **Wednesday — The Mechanism:** explainers, opinions, and case-study Fix angles. Midweek reasoning/debate.
- **Friday — The Human Close:** personal, engagement, and case-study Lesson angles. Lighter, more social close to the week.

## 3. Guardrails

- **Weeks 1–2:** no case studies, no CTAs — build trust before any proof appears.
- **Soft CTAs capped at 5 of 100 posts** (well under the 8-post ceiling), spaced ≥4 weeks apart, never in weeks 1–2 or the final post, never back-to-back. Each CTA is one soft closing line pointing to the free 30-minute revenue review call — never "DM me," never a hard pitch.
- No two Pillar-B (case study) posts back-to-back. ≥4 weeks between posts referencing the same case study.
- Pillar F: zero business mentions, no exceptions. Pillar E: identity/philosophy context only, never a pitch.
- Company name kept out of post copy entirely — the personal profile already carries that association; nothing here needs to say "subsecond" to work.
- Banned: "DM me," "link in bio," hype emoji stacks, labeling posts "case study"/"portfolio," unsupported superlatives.
- The one real testimonial (medical clinic owner) is used exactly once (Post 70), never paired with a CTA in the same post.
- Post 100 closes on real personal philosophy — never a CTA.
- **Pillar E honesty rule:** posts marked `[TEMPLATE]` are skeletons for the user to fill in with real, specific, non-fabricated detail before posting — they are not ready to publish as written. Posts marked `(real)` use only philosophy language already published on the live site and are safe to post as-is.
