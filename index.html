04Work

Selected results

Five leaks, found and closed. Each one started with traffic the business already had — and inquiries that never arrived.

  • Beauty clinic

    Interactive flat pricing

    Pricing UX · Booking

    +44% online bookings in 90 days

    The leak Prices for 120+ treatments were spread across PDFs and sub-pages. Anyone who wanted the cost of one procedure had to call and ask — and most never did.

    The fix One flat, searchable price list. Every procedure and specialist on a single page, with booking one tap away from any line.

    • 5 → 1 clicks to book a visit
    • 120+ prices on one page

  • Property law firm

    Drafting & review app on a private model

    Custom app · Private AI

    30+ hrs recovered every week

    The leak Every deal meant retyping the same six documents, then reading them line by line for the errors that cost money: a deposit that contradicts the contract, a handover date that lands on a Sunday.

    The fix An app that drafts from the deal file and checks each contract against the firm’s own playbook. The model runs on the firm’s server — no client data leaves the office.

    • 45 → 6 min per first draft
    • 6 document types automated
    • 100% on-premise, nothing sent out

  • Medical clinic

    Site redesign & booking flow

    Redesign · Conversion

    +58% online appointment bookings in 90 days

    The leak The old site buried the one thing patients came for. Walls of text, no clear path to a visit, and nothing that matched the standard of care inside the clinic.

    The fix Rebuilt around the appointment: services grouped the way patients ask for them, photography of the actual rooms and team, and booking within reach on every screen.

    • +41% avg. time on site
    • −34% bounce rate
    • 2 clicks from any page to a booking

  • Medical alert devices

    Device and plan configurator

    Configurator UX · Performance

    +27% product page conversion in 90 days

    The leak Picking a device and a care plan ran across separate screens, and the real monthly cost only appeared at checkout. People who wanted the watch left before they understood what they were signing up for.

    The fix One configurator: device, plan length and the 24/7 responder on a single screen, with the total updating as you choose. The front end was rebuilt so nothing reloads between choices.

    • 5 → 2 clicks to a complete order
    • 2.8 → 0.8 s load time
    • +18% plans attached to a device

  • B2B SaaS

    Speed rebuild on the conversion path

    Performance · Conversion

    +34% conversion rate in 90 days

    The leak The pages built to convert were the slowest on the site. Every extra second of load was paid for in signups that never happened.

    The fix Rebuilt the critical render path — 69% faster load, sub-second LCP, and nothing left blocking the main thread.

    • 0.7 s LCP
    • 0.11 CLS
    • 0 ms total blocking time
    • 96 Lighthouse performance
